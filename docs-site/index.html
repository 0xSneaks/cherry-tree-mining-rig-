SOLANA · PLANTING SIMULATION · FUNDED SOL REWARDS

THE CHERRY TREE is a Solana-native mining simulation. Users buy non-redeemable seeds, receive a share of a transparently funded SOL reward stream, and may claim rewards or compound them into additional seeds.

SIMULATION

This is a simulation. Seeds do not mine SOL, participate in Solana consensus, or represent a deposit or redeemable principal.

Overview

Plain English

THE CHERRY TREE is a game loop built around Seeds and a funded reward stream. A purchase creates production capacity; it does not fund rewards owed to other users. Rewards exist only when an authorized funder deposits SOL into the reward vault and sets an emission window.

Seeds cannot be sold back or redeemed. Users can claim settled rewards or compound them into whole additional Seeds.

Technical detail

Three program-owned vaults are separate: CAPITAL receives non-fee purchase proceeds and is locked in this version; REWARD receives fund_rewards deposits and pays claim/compound; FEE receives purchase fees and is the only vault with an authority withdrawal path. Accounting lives in crates/rigs-engine/; Anchor handlers validate accounts and move lamports.
