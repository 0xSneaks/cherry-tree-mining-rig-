fund it (CAPITAL pays nothing; FEE never reaches REWARD). A high advertised rate at low participation is cheap;
holding it as participation grows is not — the rate falls hyperbolically with planted seeds.

## 5. Refill preview hazard

`fund_rewards` re-spreads leftover + new amount over the NEW duration. In scenario D the 5 SOL top-up at day 30 with
a 365-day duration changes the rate by -56.43% (lowersRate = true). Always run the
preview (`app/scripts/ops/runway-report.ts --preview <sol> <days>`) and announce the resulting rate before funding.
Scenario B shows the same effect in a milder form: a 25 SOL / 90-day refill at day 150 extends runway but the per-day
rate drops from 0.5555 to 0.4629 SOL. Choose the duration from the rate you intend to announce, not the other way round.
